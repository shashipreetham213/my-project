# The Data Lens

**Research & Intelligence — India and the World**

The Data Lens is a data-led research and visualisation publication covering:
- Markets and investments
- Politics and governance
- Economy and banking
- World affairs and geopolitics
- Environment and nature
- Business, society and demographics
- Interactive dashboards and research reports

## Website structure

- `index.html` — homepage and editorial landing page
- `styles.css` — responsive hybrid design system
- `app.js` — navigation and dashboard preview interactions
- `assets/` — add locally hosted images, icons and downloadable assets here
- `dashboards/` — publish standalone interactive dashboards here
- `research/` — publish long-form research pages here
- `data/` — store curated CSV/JSON datasets with source and update notes

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Open the repository **Settings**.
2. Select **Pages** in the sidebar.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
4. Select branch **main** and folder **/(root)**, then save.
5. Wait for GitHub Pages to finish publishing.

## Research publishing standards

Every published analysis should include:
1. A clear title, summary and publication date.
2. The geography and time period covered.
3. Primary sources and direct source links.
4. Definitions, units, calculations and methodology.
5. Clear labels for official statistics, estimates and interpretation.
6. A last-updated date and revision note where relevant.

Do not label sample or illustrative values as live data. Market data may be delayed and should be sourced and timestamped. This publication is informational research, not investment advice.
