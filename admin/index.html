IMPORT GUIDANCE

Keep your data trustworthy

  • Keep one clear header row in CSV and Excel files
  • Use consistent date formats and units
  • Include source, geography and reporting period
  • Do not mix percentages, amounts and counts in one field
  • Review validation warnings before publishing analysis
