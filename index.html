05 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Curiosity, backed
by evidence.

The Data Lens is an independent research and visualisation project focused on making complex public information easier to understand. Its coverage spans India and the wider world — from financial markets and public policy to the economy, geopolitics and the natural environment.

The site is an informational research publication, not investment advice or a substitute for official records. Data may be revised by its original publisher; always consult the cited source for decisions.
